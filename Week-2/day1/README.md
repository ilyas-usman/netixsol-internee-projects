When should you use a bar chart vs. a histogram?
we use bar charts when we ha discrete categorical values like numbers of different students in different subject while histogram is used to make intervals of continuous data and each bin touches other.

What does a boxplot show that a histogram doesn’t?
A boxplot shows the median, Q1, Q3, whiskers, and outliers of a dataset, while a histogram only shows the distribution of continuous data in intervals (bins).

How do you add labels, title, and legend to a Matplotlib chart?
plt.xlabel("Days")
plt.ylabel("Time")
plt.title("Graph")
plt.legend()

What’s a heatmap typically used for in EDA?
In EDA, a heatmap is used to find the correlation between numeric columns. It works best with numerical data, and colors make it easy to identify which variables are strongly related.
