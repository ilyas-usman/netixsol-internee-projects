DHA Phase 9 - Payment Plan Overview

DHA Phase 9 plots are typically sold on a 1-year installment plan for balloted files:
25% down payment, followed by 4 equal quarterly installments of 18.75% each. Unlike
Bahria's plan, DHA does not charge a separate possession fee for balloted (non-possession)
files - possession fees only apply once a specific plot number has been allotted and
physical possession is being handed over, at which point a one-time development
surcharge applies based on the plot's category (Category A/B/C, based on location within
the phase).

Transfer of file ownership before full payment is completed is allowed but requires DHA's
no-objection certificate and a transfer fee, typically 1% of the plot's current market value.
