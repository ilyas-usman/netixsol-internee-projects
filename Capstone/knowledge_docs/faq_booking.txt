Frequently Asked Questions - Booking Process

Q: How do I book a property I've seen on a call with your agent?
A: Booking requires a signed booking form and the down payment amount (see the relevant
payment plan document for the property's society). Payment can be made via bank transfer,
pay order, or cash deposit directly into the developer's or seller's designated account.
We do not accept booking payments into any personal account of an individual agent.

Q: What is your cancellation and refund policy?
A: For developer-financed plots (new bookings), cancellation within 7 days of booking is
eligible for a full refund minus a 2% processing fee. After 7 days, refunds are subject to
the specific developer's policy, which varies by project - our team will confirm the exact
terms for your specific property before you book.

Q: Can I reschedule a property visit?
A: Yes, visits can be rescheduled any time before the confirmed slot by informing your
assigned agent at least 3 hours in advance. There is no charge for rescheduling.

Q: Do you charge a commission as the agency?
A: For resale/secondary market properties, our standard commission is 1% of the sale price
from each side (buyer and seller), payable at the time the sale deed is executed. For
new developer-financed bookings, there is no separate commission charged to the buyer.
