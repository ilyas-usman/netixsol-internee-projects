Gulberg, Lahore - Area Overview

Gulberg is one of Lahore's most established and centrally located commercial and residential
areas, known for its mix of high-end retail (Liberty Market, MM Alam Road), corporate offices,
and a mature residential core with mature trees and wide roads. It sits close to major
arteries connecting to Gaddafi Stadium, the Canal Road, and DHA, making commute times to
most parts of the city relatively short compared to newer suburban societies.

Commercial rents on MM Alam Road and Liberty Market are among the highest in the city due
to consistently high footfall from both local shoppers and corporate lunch-hour traffic.
Residential plots and houses in Gulberg's older blocks command a premium due to land scarcity,
since most of the area is already built out - new construction is typically redevelopment of
existing plots rather than fresh development.
