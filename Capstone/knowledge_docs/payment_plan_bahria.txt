Bahria Town Lahore - Payment Plan Overview

For residential plots in Bahria Town Lahore, the standard developer payment plan requires
a 20% down payment at the time of booking. The remaining 80% is payable in equal quarterly
installments spread over 3 years (12 installments total). Possession charges, equivalent to
5% of the total plot price, are payable separately once the development work in that phase
is substantially complete and possession is offered.

Late installment payments incur a surcharge of 2% per month on the overdue amount. Buyers
who wish to pay the full remaining balance early (before the 3-year term ends) are eligible
for a 3% early-payment rebate on the outstanding amount, provided the request is made in
writing to the developer's accounts office at least 15 days before the intended payment date.

Membership and development charges are separate from the plot price and are billed annually
starting from the second year of ownership.
