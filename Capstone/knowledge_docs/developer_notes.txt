Developer Reputation Notes

Bahria Town: One of Pakistan's largest private real estate developers, with a long track
record of project delivery across Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, and Rawalpindi. Generally
regarded as reliable for possession timelines in mature phases, though newer phases can
see delays. Strong resale demand due to brand recognition and developed infrastructure.

DHA (Defence Housing Authority): A quasi-military-affiliated developer present in most
major Pakistani cities. Considered among the most trusted developers due to institutional
backing and strict transfer/verification processes, which reduces fraud risk for buyers.
Plot prices in DHA phases tend to appreciate steadily but plans can be slower to develop
compared to some private developers.

Buyers should always verify a project's NOC (No Objection Certificate) status with the
relevant development authority before booking in any new or under-development society,
regardless of developer reputation.
